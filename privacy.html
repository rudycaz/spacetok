About SpaceTok

SpaceTok creates educational videos about space, science, engineering, and technology.

Information We Process

When users authorize TikTok integration, SpaceTok may process authorization tokens and account information required for permitted publishing functions.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, generate content, and perform publishing actions authorized by account owners.

Data Storage

Application data is stored locally where possible. Access credentials are intended to remain private. Personal information is not sold to advertisers.

Third-Party Services

SpaceTok may use OpenAI for script generation, NASA for media retrieval, and TikTok APIs for authorized video publishing.
